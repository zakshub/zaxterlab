# ZaxterLab

**Adaptive, Reusable AI Company Operating System**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**ZaxterLab** is an adaptive, reusable AI Company Operating System engineered to evolve organically—mirroring real human organizations. Rather than starting as a sprawling, pre-allocated swarm of speculative AI agents, ZaxterLab starts with a single general worker, expands to a second paired worker for oversight, and grows into specialist clusters, team leads, and departments **only when validated workload, recurring backlogs, or distinct skill boundaries strictly demand it**.

ZaxterLab treats organizational structure as a function of continuous, measurable demand rather than architectural vanity.

---

## 2. Core Philosophy & Architectural Pillars

1. **Organic Organizational Evolution**:
   - **Step 1**: One single, accountable worker delivering verifiable tasks.
   - **Step 2**: A second worker establishing an independent review and quality control loop.
   - **Step 3**: A small team with clear division of labor once single-worker queues saturate.
   - **Step 4**: Specialized departments, leads, and routing coordination only when justified by quantified metrics.

2. **Strict Independence from ZEDCU**:
   - ZaxterLab is an entirely independent codebase and runtime.
   - **Zero dependency**: Does not share ZEDCU's database, queues, state, credentials, server infrastructure, or deployment pipelines.
   - **Conceptual Reuse Only**: Borrows the naming convention, role archetypes, and Marvel-inspired organizational metaphors purely as reference templates.

3. **Strict Zero-Authorized Spend Default**:
   - Zero dollars authorized by default.
   - Operates on local models or existing free-tier quota adapters.
   - Hard programmatic locks prevent any paid API usage or external resource provisioning without explicit owner pre-approval.

4. **Human Owner Sovereignty**:
   - The human owner (Zak) holds final, unbypassable authority.
   - High-impact and consequential actions (spending, credential modification, production deployments, system mutations) fail closed without explicit sign-off.

5. **Separation of Definition from Execution**:
   - Agent definitions (persona, skills, permissions, constraints) are declarative, static specifications.
   - Agent execution sessions are ephemeral, state-tracked, and disposable.

6. **Provider-Neutral Model Abstraction**:
   - Pluggable interfaces for local models (e.g. Ollama/vLLM), free tiers, and future API endpoints without vendor lock-in.
